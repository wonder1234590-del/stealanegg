FAQ
How do I receive my item? ▾
After payment, open the order chat and message the seller with your in-game nickname. The seller will deliver the item inside the game.
What if the seller doesn't respond? ▾
Open the order and tap "⚠️ Problem" to dispute. Support will review the case and resolve it.
Can I get a refund? ▾
Yes, if the seller fails to deliver the item, support can refund your Stars after investigation.
